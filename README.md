# Fallout 4 Takeover

**Portal 2** × Fallout 4 · visual · idea by the team

Bring Fallout 4 into the game in the most visible and funny way. The change shows in the first 30 seconds of play.

> I turned a Portal 2 test chamber into a Fallout wasteland: a Nuka-pop bot torches radroaches, and I dropped a mini nuke through a portal.

## Install

Copy the mod folders plus the kit's game folder into a new folder named sigf inside your Portal 2 install, then start Portal 2 with the options -game sigf +sv_cheats 1 +map sp_a2_laser_chaining and watch the chamber change.
