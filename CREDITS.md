# Credits: Fallout 4 Takeover (Portal 2 mod)

All content is original and generated for this mod. Nothing is taken from Fallout 4 or from any other game; Portal 2's own
chamber, portal gun and engine are used as the host.

## 3D models (made for this mod)
Models: bobble (giant mascot bobblehead), crate (Vault-Tec supply crate), handy (butler bot), roach (radroach),
nuka (soda bottle), fatman (mini nuke), cap (bottle cap), mushroom (mushroom cloud), rift (ring gate).
- Source: generated with fal.ai (Nano Banana 2 image generation, BiRefNet background removal, Trellis 2 image-to-3D).
- Author: generated by the mod author's pipeline; license: original / no third-party assets.
- Converted into Portal 2 model files by the kit tool `kits/portal2/mkmdl.py` (own code, no ripped data).

## Sound effects, voices and music (made for this mod)

- Source: generated with fal.ai (sound effect, music and text-to-speech models); original, no ripped assets.
